Frequently Asked Questions

Know How Affsense Works Before You Start

Transparent rules and clear opportunity details are part of the platform experience.

Is Affsense free to join?

Yes. The planned member account is free. Individual campaigns or earning opportunities may have eligibility, geographic, traffic-source or verification requirements.

What commission can I earn from digital products?

The current planned model is 80% on eligible front-end digital product sales and 50% on eligible upsells. Each listing should show its exact commission and terms before promotion.

What are CPA offers?

CPA campaigns pay for a specified qualifying action, which may be an approved lead, registration, sale or another defined conversion. Payout and traffic rules vary by offer.

What kind of paid tasks can appear?

Eligible tasks can include legitimate content creation, research, product or landing-page feedback, testing, graphics and similar digital work. Affsense should not permit fake reviews, spam manipulation or deceptive engagement tasks.

What is the Offer Wall?

The Offer Wall can display integrated third-party opportunities. Availability and rewards may vary by country, provider and user eligibility, and credit is subject to successful tracking and validation.

How does the referral reward work?

Under the planned one-level model, members can receive a 10% lifetime reward on the defined eligible product sales generated by direct referrals. The exact eligible sales base and exclusions should be stated in the published compensation terms.

When do payouts happen?

The current plan is weekly payouts with a $20 minimum threshold. Earnings can remain pending while conversions, tasks, refunds or fraud checks are being validated.

Does Affsense guarantee income?

No. Earnings depend on actual qualifying activity, conversions, traffic, task availability, eligibility and other factors. No specific income or result is guaranteed.
